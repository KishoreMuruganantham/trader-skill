## THE 3 WORTH ADDING

### BLS.NS - BLS International Services - Rs 220.44

- THE SINGLE NUMBER THAT MAKES IT INTERESTING: ROE of about 32% (3-year average 32.6% per Screener) available at a trailing PE of 12.7x.
- THE BUSINESS: BLS is one of the top two global outsourcers of visa, passport, consular and attestation services, working for more than 46 government clients across 80-plus countries with an estimated 17% share of the global visa-outsourcing market by value in 2024 (search: Screener, Wikipedia, company annual report). The moat is the embedded government contract and compliance infrastructure, plus a negative working capital model where embassy receivables are collected ahead of obligations (search: company FY26 annual report). Promoter holding is 70.4% with 0.00% pledged (search: Trendlyne shareholding, Sep 2026). FY26 statutory and secretarial audit reports carried no qualifications (search: AGM/annual report filings).
- THE BIGGEST RISK: Contract renewal concentration and structure. The company says major contracts worth USD 1-2 billion are due for renewal soon (search: FY26 annual report), Q2 FY27 revenue grew 32% but profit only 8.6% (search: FlashFinance), and the auditor relies on other auditors for foreign subsidiaries and on management-certified data for 58 smaller subsidiaries (search: AGM disclosure). A step-down subsidiary, Atyati Technologies, changed auditor in September 2026; the outgoing firm, S.R. Batliboi, stated the reason was an independence conflict from EY-group non-audit services after the ownership change, not a disagreement over accounts, and the FY26 audit was completed and unqualified (search: NSE filing dated 29 Sep 2026). That is a flag to watch, not a proven fraud.

### APTUS.NS - Aptus Value Housing Finance - Rs 191.33

- THE SINGLE NUMBER THAT MAKES IT INTERESTING: Capital adequacy ratio of 71.04% at 31 March 2026 (search: company annual report) against a 9.3x PE from the file. This is a lender that can grow without diluting or leveraging up.
- THE BUSINESS: Aptus is a Chennai-based affordable-housing finance company lending primarily to first-time, low-income, self-employed customers in small towns, with a 339-branch network across 6 states and 1 UT, LTV around 40% and ROA around 7.7% to 7.9% (search: company FY26 annual report and Q2 FY27 call). Housing GNPA is 1.48% with credit cost of about 25 bps, AUM grew 20% YoY to over Rs 14,000 crore and profit grew 18% in Q2 FY27 (search: Yahoo Finance transcript and company results). Zero promoter pledge (search: Trendlyne and BSE shareholding, Sep 2026).
- THE BIGGEST RISK: The non-housing MSME NBFC book is deteriorating. Its GNPA rose to 2.17%, stage 2 to 6.88%, credit cost to roughly 159-169 bps, 30+ DPD to 7.08% and collection efficiency slipped to 98.35%, forcing management to raise FY27 credit-cost guidance by 5-10 bps (search: Q2 FY27 earnings call). Also, promoter holding fell from about 53% to 23.86% because WestBridge sold its 28.59% stake in FY26 (search: annual report and Trendlyne); the founder is still 19.20% but the economic alignment is weaker than it looks.

### CANFINHOME.NS - Can Fin Homes - Rs 796.40

- THE SINGLE NUMBER THAT MAKES IT INTERESTING: Gross NPA of 0.85% at FY26 end, the fifth straight quarter of declining delinquency (search: ICICI Direct FY26 result note and company FY26 report), at a 9.2x PE from the file.
- THE BUSINESS: Can Fin Homes is a 38-year-old deposit-taking HFC promoted by Canara Bank, which holds 29.99%, with 274 branches across 21 states and UTs, a Rs 42,209 crore loan book of which about 83% is housing and 68% is salaried customers (search: company investor presentation dated 14 Oct 2026 and FY26 annual report). FY26 PAT was Rs 1,086 crore, up 27%, capital adequacy 23.15%, debt/equity improved to 6.40x from 6.96x, and it has paid dividends every year since inception (search: company FY26 report). No promoter pledge is reported (search: company shareholding pattern, Sep 2026).
- THE BIGGEST RISK: Growth and rate sensitivity. AUM grew only 10.44% in FY26 and NIM is around 3.75% to 4.14% (search: ICICI Direct and Quartr), so the RBI repo at 5.50% with calibrated tightening (file) keeps funding costs from falling and caps margin expansion. ICRA notes that a stake sale by Canara Bank or a change in its support philosophy would hurt the ratings (search: ICRA rationale, 9 Jul 2026).

## THE 3 MOST TRAP-LIKE

### 1. KARURVYSYA.NS - the multiple has already re-rated past its own history

The bank is good, the price is the problem. PE is 12.5x to 12.6x now against a 5-year average of 7.38x (search: MarketFriction) or 8.11x (search: Smart Investing) and a FY22 low of 5.50x (search: StockAnalysis). P/B is 2.58x against a 5-year average of 1.12x (search: MarketFriction). The stock is at 94% of its 52-week range (file) after a 5-year price return of about 757% (search: MarketFriction and Shreni). At 2.4x to 2.6x book for a regional bank with 19% ROE and 1.9% promoter holding (search: Screener and MarketFriction), the easy money has been made. A normalisation to its own 5-year PE would imply a price near Rs 302 (search: Trendlyne), about 21% below the file price.

### 2. DEEPAKNTR.NS - earnings quality has collapsed while the multiple has not

The file shows ROE 17.6% and PE 19.5, but search data shows current ROE of about 9.4% to 10% (Screener, INDmoney, Tijori, all accessed late Oct/Nov 2026) and trailing PE of 20x to 28x. Five-year profit CAGR is -7% and three-year profit CAGR is -13%, while five-year ROE averaged 18% and last year was 10% (search: Screener). The 5-year PE range is 8.33x to 49.01x (search: INDmoney), so today's multiple is mid-to-high range on depressed earnings. Management is funding a Rs 11,500 crore project at a 60:40 debt-to-equity ratio (search: Q1 FY27 earnings call), which ends the current zero-debt, Rs 1,450 crore cash position (search: Finology). Promoter is 49.39% with 0.00% pledge (search: Finology), so governance is not the issue, the earnings are.

### 3. PETRONET.NS - a toll concession at its 52-week high with tariffs being cut

The stock sits at 91% of its 52-week range (file). The PE of 10.6x (file) is not cheap against its own history: 5-year average 10.04x, 9.5-year median 12.5x but current at the 13th percentile, and P/B 2.22x against a 5-year average of 2.11x (search: MarketFriction). ROE has fallen from a 5-year average of 22.44% to 18.56% (search: MarketFriction), and broker estimates cut FY27 ROE to 15.5% and FY28 to 14.7% (search: Motilal Oswal, 5 May 2026). On tariffs, Petronet offered GAIL 52.05 Rs/mn Btu versus 66.06 Rs/mn Btu at the expanded Dahej capacity, a discount of more than 25% (search: Argus Media), and Motilal Oswal assumes a 5% Dahej tariff cut in FY28 (search). On concessions, the Dahej concession agreement is dated 20 December 2005 with Gujarat Maritime Board and the Government of Gujarat, the 7.5 MMTPA GSPA runs to 2028 and most regasification contracts to 2036, the Kochi GSPA to 2035 and the Exxon Mobil link to 2040-41 (search: company MOA, annual report and RPT filings). Revenue fell 34.6% YoY in the Sep 2026 quarter (search: MarketFriction) and the company is funding both a Gopalpur terminal and a petrochemical complex. This is a good monopoly asset, but you are paying an average multiple for falling returns and known tariff resets.

- Reason not to own: Q2 FY26 revenue growth was only 1.5%, headcount grew 3.4%, utilisation fell to 74.9%, attrition rose to 23.4% and new deal wins fell to USD 31m from USD 41m (search: Investing.com earnings transcript). Tijori flags AI-driven 10-15% pricing compression as a bear case (search).

### LUPIN.NS
- Business/moat: Global generics and specialty pharma with 33 subsidiaries, building ophthalmology and peptide/CDMO capabilities (search: company integrated report).
- ROE: 23.9% in the file. FY26 PAT up 62% to Rs 5,355 crore and EPS Rs 116.75 (search: company FY26 results). Clean 5-year ROE series not verified.
- Balance sheet: Net debt is negative Rs 4,635 crore, net debt/equity -0.21 (search: FY26 results). Strong.
- Promoter/pledge: 46.84% Sep 2026, 0.00% pledged (search: PositoAI and IPO Plus).
- Auditor/regulator: The November 2017 USFDA warning letter for Goa and Pithampur Unit 2 has been resolved (search: company announcement, 21 Nov 2026, and FDA.Watch). A fresh Form 483 with one observation was issued at Goa after 19-23 Oct 2026 inspection (search: NSE filing 24 Oct 2026). FY26 filing shows 8 Form 483s with 29 observations across 9 inspections (search: company integrated report). No auditor qualification found.
- Valuation: PE 17.4 in file; 5-year PE average not verified.
- Reason not to own: US generics remain lumpy and compliance-heavy, and the "Agilent acquisition" in the brief could not be verified (see last section).

### POWERGRID.NS
- Business/moat: Central transmission utility and regulated monopoly under the CERC multi-year tariff framework, with assured 15.5% post-tax ROE on approved capital if availability stays above 98% (search: Koalagains and ICICI Direct). FY26 availability was 99.84% (search: ICICI Direct).
- ROE: 15.7% file, 15.75% trailing, but 5-year average 18% and 10-year 17% (search: Yahoo Finance and Screener). Five-year sales and profit CAGR only 3%, 3-year profit -2% (search: Screener).
- Balance sheet: Debt/equity 1.46x to 1.58x (search: Yahoo Finance and Tijori). Free cash flow is negative Rs 1.43 lakh crore (search: Yahoo Finance). FY26 capex Rs 39,967 crore and FY27 guidance Rs 37,000 crore (search: ICICI Direct).
- Promoter/pledge: Government-owned; insiders hold 52.91% (search: Yahoo Finance). Tijori says promoters have not pledged a significant portion; exact percentage not verified.
- Auditor/regulator: No qualification found, but Screener flags possible interest capitalisation and a low tax rate (search). Tariff regulation is the live risk: it won 10 of 14 competitive TBCB projects auctioned in H2 (search: Finology), which can mean winning at thin returns.
- Valuation: Trailing PE 14.78x, forward 13.34x, P/B 2.24x; Tijori says it trades at a discount to 3-year valuation (search). 5-year PE average not verified.
- Reason not to own: Regulated returns are falling as the asset base grows, and capex is consuming all cash, so the dividend is the only near-term return.

### INDUSTOWER.NS
- Business/moat: India's largest tower company with 270,480 macro towers and 440,153 co-locations, but only four telecom customers remain and Airtel is both 51% owner and the dominant anchor tenant (search: company Q2 FY27 release, CCI order and Angel One).
- ROE: Post-tax ROE fell to 18.8% in Q2 FY27 from 29.0% a year earlier; pre-tax 25.3% from 38.4% (search: company press release). Not holding up.
- Balance sheet: ICRA notes strong liquidity and financial flexibility; RoW rules 2024 now implemented (search: ICRA and Q1 FY27 call). Exact debt figure not verified.
- Promoter/pledge: Bharti Airtel about 51% after buying more shares in Feb 2026 (search: Angel One). Pledge percentage not verified.
- Auditor/regulator: CCI approved Airtel crossing 50% in 2024 (search: CCI order). No auditor qualification found. Hexacom related-party approval was renewed at Rs 2,000 crore per year, down from Rs 2,200 crore (search: BTW.Media, 17 Oct 2026).
- Valuation: PE 14.1 in file; 5-year PE average not verified.
- Reason not to own: FY25 profit was inflated by a roughly Rs 50.8 billion reversal of provisions when one customer cleared overdues; FY26 EBITDA fell 14% and PAT fell 28% on restated numbers, and Q2 FY27 PAT fell 2.5% while sharing revenue per tower fell 2.0% YoY (search: company annual report and Q2 release). It bought 16,100 towers from Airtel/Hexacom for Rs 3,308.7 crore (search: Capacity and DCD). This is a concentration story with deteriorating unit economics.
