# Valuation verdict: 35 Indian listed stocks

Date: 24 Nov 2026. Prices in whole rupees.

## Sources and method

- From `analysis_brief.md` (Yahoo Finance snapshot, no adjustment or restatement): price, trailing PE, forward PE, P/B, ROE, earnings growth, debt/equity, 52 week range, analyst target and consensus. All prices, P/B, ROE, growth figures and analyst upside percentages in the table below come from this file.
- From web search: five year average PE, recent one-off items, auditor opinions, regulatory action, and analyst target revisions. Search sources included Trendlyne buy/sell zone pages, Smart-Investing.in fiscal-year PE series, Wisesheets, Economic Times, Moneycontrol, Business Standard, company investor decks and SEBI/RBI reporting. Where a five year average PE could not be verified, the cell says "n/a, not verified". No number was invented.
- PEG in the table is trailing PE divided by the earnings growth figure in the data file. It is a rough growth check, not a forecast. Where earnings are negative, falling, or up only because of a collapsed prior-year base, PEG is marked n/a.
- Bank and NBFC verdicts use P/B against ROE and PE against the banking sector. Industrial and consumer verdicts use PE against the stock's own five year average, plus cash generation where available. IT verdicts use PE against growth.
- Analyst upside is target price divided by price, minus one, using the data file target. These are consensus figures and several are stale or in the process of being cut.

## Notes on misleading trailing PE

- TMPV.NS and TATACHEM.NS have no meaningful trailing PE because earnings are negative. Do not read the forward PE as a valuation; both depend on a turnaround.
- INDUSINDBK.NS has a positive trailing PE only because of a depressed profit base after the derivatives accounting scandal. Book value 1.1x on 4.5% ROE is the more informative number.
- VOLTAS.NS, DRREDDY.NS and NATCOPHARM.NS have high or low trailing PEs driven by one quarter or one year effects, see the one-off notes below.
- SUNPHARMA.NS trailing PE is depressed by one-off charges, so the stock is cheaper than the headline 41x suggests.
- PEG figures for TITAN.NS, TATACONSUM.NS, EXIDEIND.NS, MANAPPURAM.NS and THANGAMAYL.NS use growth rates that are base effects rather than sustainable trends. Treat them as flags, not as valuation evidence.

## Recent one-offs and governance notes found by search

- TCS, INFY, WIPRO, HCLTECH, TECHM: brokers have been cutting targets on AI disruption and pricing pressure. CLSA has a Rs 2038 target on TCS. Morgan Stanley cut Infosys to equal weight at Rs 1740. One broker cut HCL Tech to Rs 1000 from Rs 1370 and Wipro to Rs 160 from Rs 200. Kotak still values TCS at Rs 3090 and Infosys at Rs 1530, so the street is split.
- TATASTEEL.NS: Q2 FY27 net profit Rs 3700 cr included a Rs 349 cr net exceptional gain, mainly a revaluation gain on the TMILL stake. Auditor qualified the opinion on Netherlands asset values.
- TMPV.NS: Q2 FY27 net loss Rs 5531 cr included Rs 6738 cr of exceptional items, largely employee separation of Rs 3776 cr and asset impairment. The prior-year quarter had a Rs 76170 cr gain, which makes year on year comparisons useless.
- SUNPHARMA.NS: Q2 FY27 net profit fell 36% to Rs 1989 cr because of Rs 1269 cr of exceptional items, including Rs 923 cr for the proposed Organon acquisition and a US antitrust settlement. Adjusted profit before tax rose 6%.
- NATCOPHARM.NS: Q2 FY27 profit down 61% to Rs 203 cr, revenue down 29% to Rs 967 cr on lower lenalidomide. One-off PPA amortisation and a VRS charge at an Adcock facility.
- TATACHEM.NS: Q4 FY26 impairment of Rs 1837 cr on US goodwill plus about Rs 160 to 182 cr of deferred tax asset write-off. Results are loss making on a trailing basis.
- TATACONSUM.NS: Q2 FY27 exceptional charge of Rs 74 lakh on contingent consideration for an acquisition; standalone PAT fell because of Rs 64 cr of exceptional items.
- INDUSINDBK.NS: derivatives portfolio overvalued by about 2.35% of net worth, roughly Rs 1960 to 2000 cr. Former CEO terminated and clawback invoked; deputy CEO resigned. The clearest governance red flag in the file.
- MUTHOOTFIN.NS: RBI penalty of Rs 5.8 lakh for risk management failures. Stage-2 loans at 12.5% of the portfolio and falling gold prices are the live risk.
- MANAPPURAM.NS: Stage-2 loans rose from 0.5% to about 5%; gold loan growth of 92% is strong but the asset quality trend is the concern.
- THANGAMAYL.NS: no active promoter pledging found, auditor reports unmodified. The risk here is leverage and gold prices, not governance.
- For the remaining smaller or less liquid names (TATATECH, ZYDUSWELL, EXIDEIND, ARE&M, FEDERALBNK, SOUTHBANK, TMB, TATAELXSI, NATCOPHARM), no five year average PE could be verified from the searches run, so the table says so rather than estimating one.
